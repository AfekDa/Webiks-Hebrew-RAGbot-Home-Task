The right page is usually found, but rarely first.

How the system answers a question today:
1
Trained Hebrew model turns the question into a meaning vector
2
Elasticsearch returns the 50 closest paragraphs
3
Collapse to pages; a page ranks by its single best paragraph
Step 3 is the weak spot. A page with one paragraph that happens to look like the question can outrank the page that is actually about the question.
