Page scoring

Score the page, not just its luckiest paragraph.

page score = best paragraph
  + 0.25 × second-best paragraph
  + 0.25 × title match
All three from the same trained model. A small margin gate (0.05) keeps generic "hub" pages from jumping up from far below.
Second-best paragraph
The right page usually has several matching paragraphs. A look-alike has one.
Title match
Kol-Zchut titles name the topic. Paragraphs are fragments that often don't.

Why not the obvious upgrades?
The shipped retriever was trained on these very questions. Anything that overrules it loses. Both ideas were built, integrated and tested the same way.
